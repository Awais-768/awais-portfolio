01 — About

Front-end craft, built on a decade of shipping.

I've spent more than ten years building the part of the web people actually touch. I started at P2H (PSD to HTML), where I contributed to more than 300 web projects, converting design concepts into responsive, cross-browser HTML and CSS. Since 2020 I've worked on product teams, first at LeadPops and now at DevMinified, developing and maintaining front-end applications in React.

My approach is simple: understand the design and the user, build reusable components, and keep the code clean enough to change later. I pay close attention to pixel accuracy, responsive behaviour, accessibility and load speed, and I work closely with designers, developers and QA to get features right.

06 — Process

How a project moves.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review the design, requirements and target devices. Ask questions early.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Break the UI into reusable components and agree on structure and naming.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement semantic, responsive markup and styles, matched to the design.

  4. 04

    Test & Optimize

    Check across browsers and breakpoints, fix issues, trim load time.

  5. 05

    Ship & Maintain

    Hand off through reviewed pull requests and keep improving after launch.
